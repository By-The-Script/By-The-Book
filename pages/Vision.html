Our Mission

Reinventing the Reading Experience

For decades, digital reading platforms have remained stagnant, treating books like simple static files. Meanwhile, interactive media has redefined engagement.

By The Book is building the modern home for readers—bringing depth, progression, and social connection back to literature without losing the quiet magic of a great story.

Companion AI

Meet Dr. Meow

Dr. Meow isn't just a chatbot; he's your personal reading companion, mentor, and guide through deep literary worlds.

  • 💡
    Chapter Breakdowns: Deconstruct complex prose, historical context, or dense plot points on demand.
  • 💬
    Talk To Characters: Talk with your favorite characters. Understand their motivations, backstories, and hidden secrets through interactive dialogue.
  • 🖼️
    Picture Making: See scenes from your favorite books come to life with pictures and animations.
  • 🎯
    Tailored Recommendations: Discover books that fit your exact taste, mood, and reading pace.
  • 🔥
    Daily Motivation: Stay encouraged with personalized milestones and gentle reading reminders.
  • ❓
    Interactive Quizzes: Test your comprehension and unlock lore achievements as you read.
