What We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Data Type Examples Required?
Account Info Username, email, password (hashed), display name Yes
Profile Data Bio, profile photo, chosen Dr. Meow skin Optional
Activity Data Books read, reviews written, Mice earned, XP, streaks Auto
Content Reviews, blog posts, comments, articles Optional
Technical Data Browser type, device info, IP address (via Firebase) Auto
Auth Provider Data If you sign in with Google/GitHub/Microsoft: name, email, profile picture from that provider If used
🐾 Dr. Meow's Note: We do not collect your home address, phone number, payment information, or real name unless you choose to add them to your profile. Dr. Meow is nosy about books, not about you.
02

How We Collect It

We collect data through:

  • Registration forms — when you create an account.
  • Third-party authentication providers — Google, GitHub, and Microsoft, via Firebase Authentication. When you use these, the provider shares basic profile data with us per their own privacy policies.
  • Platform activity — automatically logged when you use the site (reading activity, reviews, clicks, quiz participation).
  • Firebase services — Firestore (our database), Firebase Auth, and Firebase Hosting automatically collect certain technical data as part of their operation. See Firebase's Privacy Policy.
03

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Track your reading activity, Mice balance, XP, and streaks.
  • Deliver personalized book recommendations from Dr. Meow.
  • Display your public profile, reviews, and posts to other users.
  • Send you notifications about events, streaks, and activity (only if you opt in).
  • Improve the platform — understanding how users interact with the site helps us build better features.
  • Enforce these Terms of Use and investigate violations.

We do not:

  • Sell your data to advertisers or third parties.
  • Use your data for targeted advertising.
  • Share your data with anyone outside of the service providers listed in Section 5.
🐾 Dr. Meow's Note: By The Book is ad-free. Your data is used to make the site better for you — not to sell you cheese you didn't ask for.
04

Data Storage & Security

All data is stored securely using Google Firebase, which provides:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Hashed and salted password storage (Firebase Auth handles this — we never see your raw password).
  • Cloud infrastructure with enterprise-grade security standards.

While we take all reasonable measures to protect your data, no internet system is 100% immune to breaches. In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law.

05

Sharing Your Data

We share your data only with:

  • Google / Firebase — our backend infrastructure provider. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Authentication providers you choose — Google, GitHub, Microsoft. Only the data needed to identify you is passed to us.
  • Other users of the platform — your username, profile picture, public reviews, posts, and stats (like streak and level) are visible to other registered users. Your email is never public.

We do not share your data with any analytics companies, data brokers, advertising networks, or any other third parties.

06

Children's Privacy (COPPA) 👧👦

By The Book includes content suitable for younger readers, and we take their privacy extremely seriously.

  • Users under 13 may not register without verified parental or guardian consent.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without consent.
  • If you are a parent and believe your child has registered without your knowledge, please contact us immediately at contact@bythebook.com and we will delete the account and all associated data promptly.
  • We do not display advertising of any kind, which means younger users are never targeted with commercial content.
🐾 Dr. Meow's Note: Dr. Meow loves young readers and is very protective of them. Anyone trying to harm or exploit younger members of this community will be banned immediately and reported if required by law.

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

👁️ Right to Access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

✏️ Right to Rectification

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

🗑️ Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").

🚫 Right to Object

Object to certain types of data processing.

📦 Right to Portability

Request your data in a portable, machine-readable format.

⏸️ Right to Restrict

Request that we limit how we process your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@bythebook.com. We will respond within 30 days.

08

Cookies & Tracking

By The Book uses minimal cookies, limited to:

  • Authentication cookies — set by Firebase Auth to keep you logged in across sessions. These are strictly necessary and cannot be disabled while using the service.
  • Session storage — temporary browser storage used to cache book data and improve loading speed. This is cleared when you close the tab.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies. There is no Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar tracking on this site.

🐾 Dr. Meow's Note: The only thing tracking you here is your reading streak. And Dr. Meow. But in a supportive way.
09

Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile data, Mice balance, XP, and personal information are deleted within 30 days.
  • Public content (reviews, posts) may be retained in anonymized form.
  • Backup data may persist for up to 90 days before being fully purged from Firebase infrastructure.

Accounts that have been inactive for more than 2 years may be subject to automatic data deletion, with prior email notice where possible.

For significant changes — especially those that affect how we collect or use personal data — we will notify users via email and in-platform announcement before the changes take effect.
